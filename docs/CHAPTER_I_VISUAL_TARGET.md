# Chapter I Visual Target

Chapter I must read as a stylized Late Bronze Age coastal battle, not as a debug grid.

## Scene hierarchy

- Aegean sea on the west side with layered water bands and animated foam.
- Sandy landing beach with wet shoreline, dunes, rocks, sparse scrub and Greek landing debris.
- Two readable invasion lanes formed as worn earth tracks rather than square tiles.
- Greek ships, beach camp, standards and landing troops establish the attacking side.
- Troy occupies the east side as a fortified silhouette: wall, towers, gatehouse, banners, braziers and elevated skyline.
- Defensive structures use wood, stone, bronze and cloth silhouettes appropriate to the setting.
- Chapter opening uses a short landing shot and title card, then returns to gameplay framing.

## Readability rules

- Roads remain visible for gameplay but may not resemble a checkerboard/grid.
- Build points stay interactive without large permanent debug meshes.
- Greek faction uses cooler desaturated blue/bronze accents.
- Trojan faction uses warm red/ochre/gold accents.
- Fire and banners are focal accents near Troy; sea foam and sails are focal accents near the beach.
- Gameplay colliders and path data remain authoritative and independent from presentation geometry.
