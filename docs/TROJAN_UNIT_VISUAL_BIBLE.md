# The Troy Game — Trojan Unit Visual Bible

Status: working art-direction baseline for Chapter I and reusable Trojan roster rules.

## 1. Purpose

This document defines a single visual language for Trojan defenders so that every unit:

- reads clearly from the gameplay camera;
- communicates combat role in roughly one second;
- differs by body type, age, face, posture and silhouette rather than only equipment;
- fits the same humorous heroic-fantasy world;
- can be translated into production 3D assets without losing identity.

This is an art-direction document, not a statement that any current runtime placeholder is final.

## 2. Core visual direction

The Trojan roster uses a humorous heroic-fantasy style inspired by hand-drawn game animation: exaggerated proportions, expressive faces and poses, strong irregular shapes, clean readable shading, saturated but controlled colors, and a playful Bronze Age fantasy tone.

The target is not photorealism, not historical reconstruction, not anime, not generic modern mobile fantasy, not Pixar-like 3D, and not slapstick parody with no heroic weight.

The desired balance is **heroic + humorous + readable**.

## 3. Gameplay-camera readability rules

Because gameplay is viewed from above at an angle, the most important read zones are:

1. head and helmet;
2. shoulders and upper torso;
3. primary weapon;
4. shield or role prop;
5. cloth/plume/cape shape;
6. pose and body mass.

A unit fails visual review if it only becomes identifiable in a close-up beauty shot.

## 4. Faction language

### Troy

Trojan defenders use a warm visual family:

- red and dark red cloth;
- ochre and sun-faded fabric;
- bronze and gold metal accents;
- warm leather and wood;
- monumental, proud, defensive silhouettes.

Troy should communicate fortification, honor, sun, bronze, fire and legend.

### Greek contrast

Greek attackers should sit in a cooler family:

- blue and desaturated blue cloth;
- white / pale cloth;
- bronze with cooler surrounding colors;
- sharper, more expeditionary silhouettes.

The factions must remain readable even when several units overlap.

## 5. Body-type matrix

### Heroic Large

Tall, broad-shouldered, narrow waist, strong upright posture. Reserved for named heroes and elite figures.

Primary use: Hector.

### Standard Soldier

Medium height, sturdy but not oversized, compact military stance.

Primary use: Trojan Spearman.

### Lean Agile

Narrow torso, longer-looking limbs, lighter equipment, active posture.

Primary use: Trojan Archer.

### Heavy Broad

Very wide torso, compressed neck, heavy armor mass, planted stance.

Primary use: Trojan Guard / Shield Bearer.

### Old Thin Mystic

Shorter than soldiers, narrow shoulders, thin limbs, older face, ritual posture.

Primary use: Priest of Apollo / fire priest variants.

### Unstable Maniac

Asymmetric, hunched or forward-leaning posture, expressive face, loose accessories, dangerous energy.

Primary use: Fire Keeper / Fire Thrower.

### Giant Monster

Large scale, asymmetric anatomy, oversized hands/feet/shoulders, heavy movement, monster-first silhouette.

Primary use: Cyclops.

## 6. Chapter I Trojan roster

### 6.1 Hector — Trojan Hero

**Role:** mobile elite hero and visual benchmark for the faction.

**Body:** Heroic Large.

**Age:** mature adult, approximately 30–40.

**Character:** noble, disciplined, confident, controlled. Humor may exist in animation timing or reactions, but Hector himself should not look foolish.

**Silhouette:** tall armored body, large round shield, long spear, strong helmet shape, red cape or major red cloth mass.

**Face:** mature, strong jaw, controlled beard/stubble, focused eyes.

**Palette:** rich Trojan red, bronze, warm leather, selective gold.

**Do not:** make him a normal spearman with more decoration; make him young and soft-faced; make him barbarian-like; strip away armor; push the face into comedy.

### 6.2 Trojan Spearman

**Role:** baseline melee defender.

**Body:** Standard Soldier.

**Age:** adult, approximately 25–40.

**Character:** dependable, disciplined, ordinary professional soldier.

**Silhouette:** medium body mass, spear, readable round shield, moderate armor.

**Face:** ordinary soldier; must not reuse Hector’s hero face.

**Palette:** red/ochre cloth, bronze, leather.

**Do not:** make him as broad or decorated as Hector; make him too thin; turn him into a comic caricature with no military read.

### 6.3 Trojan Archer

**Role:** baseline ranged defender.

**Body:** Lean Agile.

**Age:** young to mature adult, approximately 20–35.

**Character:** quick, alert, slightly nervous or energetic.

**Silhouette:** narrow torso, clearly visible bow arc, visible quiver, lighter armor.

**Face:** narrower and livelier than infantry; less square and heavy.

**Palette:** warm red/ochre cloth, lighter bronze use.

**Do not:** make him a spearman without a shield; hide the bow in the torso silhouette; make him bulky.

### 6.4 Trojan Guard / Shield Bearer

**Role:** heavy defender / tank.

**Body:** Heavy Broad.

**Age:** mature veteran, approximately 30–50.

**Character:** stubborn, immovable, heavy, reliable.

**Silhouette:** very broad chest, huge shield, compressed neck, dense armor mass, planted legs.

**Face:** heavy veteran features, broader nose/jaw, thicker beard options.

**Palette:** heavy bronze, deep Trojan red, dark leather.

**Do not:** make him simply a larger spearman; make him tall and elegant; reduce the shield until it stops being the class identifier.

### 6.5 Ballista Crew

**Role:** siege support team.

The crew must read as a small cast, not duplicated soldiers.

**Master Ballistician:** thin, dry, nervous, smart, approximately 35–55; active hands; narrow face; tools or firing mechanism cues.

**Assistant:** average or sturdy build; practical worker; handles ammunition and mechanism.

**Heavy Loader (optional third silhouette):** broad, physically strong, slightly clumsy, visually funny against the nervous master.

**Do not:** use identical heads/bodies; make the crew three spearmen standing beside a ballista.

### 6.6 Priest of Apollo

**Role:** support / heal / buff / sacred magic.

**Body:** Old Thin Mystic.

**Age:** approximately 55–75.

**Character:** wise, dry, strict, elevated rather than frantic.

**Silhouette:** shorter and thinner than soldiers; robes; staff, sacred vessel or solar symbol; ritual hand pose.

**Face:** old, narrow, lined, grey/white hair and beard.

**Palette:** light ochre, cream, muted white, gold/bronze, small warm-red Trojan link.

**Do not:** give him soldier proportions; make him young; pose him like infantry.

### 6.7 Fire Keeper / Fire Thrower

**Role:** special ranged area-damage unit.

**Body:** Unstable Maniac.

**Age:** approximately 30–50.

**Character:** manic pyromaniac, dangerously enthusiastic, comic but threatening.

**Silhouette:** hunched or forward-leaning body, bottle bundles, visible flame, asymmetric straps and cloth.

**Face:** wide eyes, manic grin or tense grimace, burnt/unkempt hair or beard.

**Weapon:** thrown burning bottles; ignition source/fuse/fuel props must be readable.

**Do not:** make him a normal soldier holding a torch; make him calm or elegant; confuse him with a priest.

### 6.8 Cyclops

**Role:** elite mythic heavy thrower.

**Body:** Giant Monster.

**Character:** brute force, simple rage, heavy comic physicality.

**Silhouette:** huge body, one dominant eye, oversized arm/hand holding a boulder, asymmetry and thick legs.

**Do not:** scale up a normal human and call it a Cyclops; make him slim, symmetrical or refined.

## 7. Face diversity rules

The roster must not reuse one heroic male head with different helmets.

Vary:

- jaw width;
- nose shape and length;
- brow angle;
- eye spacing;
- beard silhouette;
- hairline;
- age lines;
- cheek mass;
- head height/width ratio.

Role examples:

- Hector: strong, noble, mature;
- Spearman: ordinary military face;
- Archer: narrower, quicker expression;
- Guard: heavy veteran face;
- Priest: old and dry;
- Fire Thrower: manic and asymmetric;
- Cyclops: monster-first facial construction.

## 8. Weapon and prop readability

Primary role props are class identifiers and must not disappear into the silhouette.

- spear = melee line defender;
- bow + quiver = ranged defender;
- giant shield = tank;
- staff / sacred vessel = support;
- burning bottles = special area thrower;
- boulder = mythic heavy thrower.

Props should be slightly exaggerated in size where needed for gameplay readability.

## 9. Animation personality

Even before final authored animation exists, every unit needs a distinct motion idea.

- Hector: controlled, confident, heroic weight.
- Spearman: disciplined, direct thrusts.
- Archer: quick draw/release rhythm.
- Guard: low center of gravity, brace and heavy recovery.
- Priest: ritual gestures and deliberate channeling.
- Fire Thrower: twitchy, overcommitted throws, delighted fire reactions.
- Cyclops: long heavy windup, full-body boulder throw, slow recovery.

## 10. Global style exclusions

Avoid:

- photoreal anatomy and materials;
- modern AAA PBR realism as the primary read;
- anime/manga face construction;
- Pixar/Disney-like 3D character treatment;
- generic mobile-fantasy clones;
- same body/face reused across classes;
- tiny realistic weapons that vanish from gameplay view;
- pure comedy with no heroic or dangerous read.

## 12. Visual definition of done

A Trojan unit is visually ready for production approval only when:

1. its role reads in about one second;
2. its silhouette works from the actual gameplay camera;
3. it does not look like another class with swapped equipment;
4. body type, age and face match this bible;
5. faction palette is clear;
6. primary weapon/prop is readable;
7. pose and animation concept reinforce the class;
8. the design works beside the rest of the Trojan roster;
9. the asset can be translated into a 3D production model without losing class identity;
10. final runtime approval still follows the repository-wide DONE gate in `MODEL_ART_INVENTORY.md`.
