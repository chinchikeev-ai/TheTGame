# TheTroyGame — Troy Defense Units & Fire Faction Identity

## 1. Canonical Faction Identity

**Troy = Fire.**

Fire is the primary elemental and visual identity of the Trojan faction.

This affects:
- unit design;
- tower-unit upgrades;
- VFX;
- projectiles;
- battlefield hazards;
- artifacts;
- Divine Powers;
- UI accents;
- late-game visual escalation.

Core visual language:
- bronze;
- dark red;
- gold;
- charcoal black;
- embers;
- burning pitch;
- solar / Apollo symbolism.

The faction should feel like a fortified bronze-age city whose defense becomes increasingly associated with heat, flame and divine solar power.

---

# 2. Tower-Unit Philosophy

Troy does not primarily build abstract fantasy towers.

The player's defenses are **Tower-Units**: fixed combat positions occupied by soldiers, crews, war machines, priests or mythological creatures.

Canonical rule:

`Build Position → Trojan Unit / Crew / Creature → Functions as a Tower`

Examples:
- an Archer Post contains visible Trojan archers;
- a Ballista position contains a real weapon and crew;
- a Fire Crew throws or pours burning pitch;
- a Cyclops physically throws massive stones;
- Scylla and Charybdis occupy unique mythological deployment positions.

This keeps the battlefield visually alive and makes the defense feel like an army rather than a collection of mechanical turrets.

---

# 3. Core Trojan Tower-Units

## 3.1 Trojan Archers

**Role:** Basic ranged DPS / anti-light.

Behavior:
- rapid ranged fire;
- prioritize light and unarmored infantry;
- reliable early-game defense.

Fire synergy:
- later upgrade to Fire Arrows;
- burning targets may receive bonus follow-up damage.

Possible progression:

`2 Archers → Veteran Archers → Fire Arrows → Royal Archers / Twin Volley`

---

## 3.2 Trojan Spear Wall

**Role:** Anti-heavy / anti-chariot.

Behavior:
- first basic build option;
- short 1.5-cell poke radius;
- medium attack rate;
- melee spear thrusts, not thrown javelins;
- high armor penetration;
- bonus damage against Heavy infantry and Chariots.

Possible progression:

`Spear Wall → Heavy Spears → Barbed Spears → Veteran Spear Guard`

Fire synergy:
- optional heated spearhead specialization;
- additional damage against burning armored enemies.

---

## 3.3 Ballista Crew

**Role:** Heavy single-target / anti-siege / boss damage.

Behavior:
- slow rate of fire;
- very high projectile damage;
- long range;
- strong armor penetration.

Primary targets:
- Heavy Hoplites;
- Siege Towers;
- Battering Rams;
- Heroes / Bosses.

Possible progression:

`Light Ballista → Reinforced Ballista → Piercing Bolt → Siege Breaker`

Fire synergy:
- heated / burning bolts at high tier;
- bonus structural damage to burning Siege units.

---

## 3.4 Fire Crew

**Role:** AoE / Burn / area denial.

This is the most explicit expression of Troy's Fire identity.

Behavior:
- throws burning pitch, oil or fire pots;
- creates burning zones on enemy routes;
- applies Burn damage over time;
- strongest against dense infantry formations.

Possible progression:

`Fire Pots → Burning Pitch → Larger Fire Zone → Inferno Crew`

Primary status:

**BURN**

Burn should be the signature Trojan combat status.

---

## 3.5 Trojan Shield Guard / Garrison

**Role:** Block / frontline control.

Unlike normal ranged Tower-Units, the Garrison deploys soldiers directly onto the road or chokepoint.

Behavior:
- physically blocks enemies;
- creates kill zones for ranged defenders;
- protects nearby positions;
- can be killed and redeployed.

Possible progression:

`2 Shield Guards → 3 Guards → Shield Wall → Royal Guard`

Fire synergy:
- enemies held by Guards remain inside burning zones longer;
- high-tier Guards gain temporary resistance while fighting near Fire effects.

---

## 3.6 Priests of Apollo

**Role:** Support / buff / debuff.

Apollo is the principal divine patron associated with Troy in the game world and reinforces Troy's solar/fire identity.

Behavior:
- buffs Trojan defenders;
- weakens enemy armor;
- slows or disrupts selected groups;
- may amplify Burn / solar effects.

Possible progression:

`Prayer → Sun Blessing → Armor Weakening → Radiance of Apollo`

Priests are support Tower-Units and should deal little direct damage.

---

# 4. Cyclops

## Cyclops Artillery

**Role:** Heavy AoE artillery.

The Cyclops replaces the previously proposed Slingers.

It is not a cheap basic unit.
It is a large mythological Tower-Unit used for powerful area damage.

Behavior:
- lifts and throws massive stones;
- slow attack speed;
- very high impact damage;
- large splash radius;
- can stagger or knock down Light enemies.

Strengths:
- dense infantry formations;
- clustered shield units;
- siege crews;
- chokepoints.

Weaknesses:
- slow reload;
- expensive;
- inefficient against isolated fast targets.

Possible progression:

`Stone Throw → Massive Boulder → Shattering Impact → Volcanic Boulder`

### Fire specialization

At high tier, the Cyclops may throw a **Volcanic Boulder**:
- impact AoE;
- Burn status;
- short-lived burning ground zone.

This preserves the Fire identity of Troy without making the Cyclops itself a fire creature.

---

# 5. Unique Mythological Tower-Units

There are exactly two unique mythological creature defenses in the current concept:

- **Scylla**;
- **Charybdis**.

They replace the previously proposed Hydra concept.

## Hard Deployment Rule

A map may contain at most:

- **1 Scylla**;
- **1 Charybdis**.

Therefore the maximum number of these unique mythological creatures active on one map is:

**2 total.**

Duplicates are forbidden.

The player cannot build:
- 2 Scyllas;
- 2 Charybdises;
- unlimited copies of either creature.

These are unique legendary entities, not standard repeatable towers.

---

# 6. Scylla

**Class:** Unique Mythological Tower-Unit.

**Role:** Multi-target DPS / elite killer.

Scylla occupies a special mythological deployment point rather than a normal small build pad.

Behavior:
- attacks several enemies simultaneously;
- multiple heads can select different targets;
- excels against dense groups and Elite units;
- can cover a broad section of a route.

Suggested base mechanic:

**Multiple Heads**

Each active head may attack a separate target.

Example progression:
- base: 3 simultaneous targets;
- upgraded: 4 targets;
- final: stronger bite / ranged lash / bleed effect.

Strengths:
- mixed enemy groups;
- Elite units;
- high-pressure lanes.

Weaknesses:
- very expensive;
- unique placement;
- limited map availability;
- less efficient against Siege structures than Ballista.

Scylla is **not Fire-aligned**.

That is intentional: unique mythological units may break Troy's normal elemental identity.

---

# 7. Charybdis

**Class:** Unique Mythological Tower-Unit.

**Role:** Area control / pull / slow / formation disruption.

Charybdis creates a localized whirlpool / vortex effect around a route section.

Behavior:
- pulls Light enemies toward the center;
- groups enemies together for AoE attacks;
- strongly slows Heavy units;
- periodically damages enemies caught in the vortex;
- creates ideal targets for Cyclops and Fire Crew attacks.

Strengths:
- crowd control;
- swarm management;
- setup for AoE combos;
- delaying dangerous pushes.

Weaknesses:
- lower direct DPS than Scylla;
- bosses resist displacement;
- unique placement;
- very expensive.

Charybdis is **not Fire-aligned**.

Its Water / Sea nature deliberately contrasts with Troy's normal Fire identity.

---

# 8. Scylla + Charybdis Synergy

The two unique creatures should form a deliberate legendary combo without becoming mandatory.

**Charybdis controls. Scylla kills.**

Example interaction:

1. Charybdis pulls a group together.
2. Enemies are slowed inside the vortex.
3. Scylla attacks multiple trapped targets.
4. Cyclops or Fire Crew can exploit the clustered formation.

This creates a high-cost late-game defense composition.

However, maps must remain beatable without owning both creatures.

---

# 9. Fire Combat System

## Signature Status: Burn

Burn is the primary elemental status associated with Trojan defenses.

Possible sources:
- Fire Arrows;
- Fire Crew;
- high-tier Ballista bolts;
- Volcanic Cyclops Boulder;
- Apollo Artifacts;
- Divine Powers;
- environmental fire.

Burn behavior:
- damage over time;
- duration refresh or stacking subject to balancing;
- clear visual readability;
- possible synergy bonuses from other Tower-Units.

Example synergies:
- Ballista: +15–20% damage vs burning Siege targets;
- Archers: higher finishing damage vs burning Light units;
- Guards: hold enemies inside burning ground;
- Priests of Apollo: increase Burn duration or lower fire resistance;
- Cyclops: spread Burn through large impact zones.

Not every unit must apply Burn. The faction should use a network of Fire interactions rather than giving every attack fire damage.

# 11. Design Principle

Troy's defensive identity should be immediately readable:

**Bronze soldiers + fortified positions + Fire + Apollo + mythological escalation.**

Early campaign:
- soldiers;
- archers;
- Ballista;
- Guards.

Mid campaign:
- Fire Crew;
- Priests;
- Cyclops.

Late campaign:
- stronger Fire synergies;
- Scylla;
- Charybdis;
- Divine Powers;
- legendary Artifacts.

The progression therefore moves naturally from believable Bronze Age defense into increasingly mythological warfare as the siege approaches its climax.
